Dictating while it works

passthrough · server-side queue

You talk while main is mid-turn. Your words go straight to main — no model in between — and wait in a queue held by the server, so they can be cancelled until main finishes its turn.

Question with options

AskUserQuestion · spoken from a template

You hear the question and the option labels; descriptions stay on screen. Say a number, a label, or “the second one” — or click. Anything else becomes the “other” answer and is read back before sending.
