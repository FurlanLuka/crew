# v7.0.3: Talk to it, read its answers

A new switch in **Settings → Voice: Spoken replies.** Turn it off and Voice OS keeps listening in
every mode, on the page and in Discord, but never reads its answers aloud: everything it would have
said shows on the page instead. Nothing is sent to be spoken, so a line shows the moment it's
ready. Like voice off, it's for the whole server and lasts across restarts.

On a phone, leaving the app and coming back no longer sets off the iPhone's mic sounds every time.
In push to talk Voice OS used to hold the mic between presses, and iOS paused and resumed it as the
app left and came back. Now it lets go of the mic when you leave, and your next press opens it again.
That first press after coming back takes a moment longer while the mic opens. A press you're in the
middle of still ends cleanly, and hands-free and on demand keep listening as before.
