# crew 5.3.0

5.2.0 broke switching in a way I hit straight away: "go into crew" or "can you check out crew?"
got sent to the session on screen as words for it, and "can you stop?" got refused as a listening
command. Going to a session is never words for it now, and stop reads as a stop again. I went through
the rest of that day's log and debug notes too. Questions like "status" or "how far are you?" about
the session on screen go to that session instead of being answered by the router. Text over 20,000
characters stays in the box with a warning where it used to vanish. And a yes to "want me to ask it?"
sends your actual question, not the word "yes".

The off-screen conversation stuff from 5.2.0 got a cleanup as well. Replies to the meanwhile line go
to the sessions it named, an update is said once, and the switch offers are down to one: "Sent to X.
Switch there?", only on your first reply to an update you heard to the end. The per-session topic is
gone because it went stale and nobody used it; tiles show the last real request instead. Long
streams open at their end and stay there now. On remotes, `crew voice remote` restarts a daemon on
another release right away instead of waiting for busy sessions to finish.
