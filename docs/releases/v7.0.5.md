# v7.0.5: A smoother swipe back

The back swipe in the app saved to an iPhone's home screen is smoother. Home, the screen it uncovers,
is now ready before your finger lands, so the first moments of the drag no longer stall while it's
drawn. It dims under a light dark layer instead of a filter the iPhone had to redraw across the
whole screen on every frame. And the screen and Home underneath move once per frame, following
exactly where your finger is.
