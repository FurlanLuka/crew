# v7.0.2: Queued messages on your phone

Messages waiting behind a session's turn now show on the phone too: "2 queued" sits just above the
bar, and tapping it lists them with **Send now** and **Cancel** for each. It only says they go after
this turn while a turn is running; words to a session that isn't active wait there as well.

The text box keeps its place. When the keyboard opened on an iPhone, the box ended up near the top
of the screen; now it sits right on the keyboard. And when you first open a session the box is at
the very bottom, instead of a little above it until something moved.

Two fixes beyond the phone. Restarting a dev server could sometimes do nothing, with nothing saying
so: the restart typed its command while the shell was still cleaning up the old one. It now waits
for the shell first. And starting a tmux session right after stopping the last one no longer fails
when crew's sessions are the only ones on the machine.
