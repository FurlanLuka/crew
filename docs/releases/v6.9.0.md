# v6.9.0: Groups in the top bar

With a lot of active sessions the top bar ran out of room. Now you can group them. Drop a tab on the
middle of another tab and name the group. It gets a colour of its own, and you can change it. The
group becomes one chip that shows its sessions' dots, the one that needs you first, its name, and how
many sessions it holds. The count turns red when one of them needs you.

Click a group and the bar holds only that group: its tabs sit inside its colour, with **‹** at the
end to bring the rest back. Esc closes it too. If a session outside the group needs you, a red dot on
**‹** says so. Opening a session outside the group, or activating one, closes it, so the bar never
hides what you're looking at.

Right-click a tab, or long-press it on a phone, to add it to a group, start one with another tab, or
take it out. Right-click a group to open, rename, recolour or ungroup it. Drop a tab on the middle of
a group to add it, or near the edge of any tab to reorder, as before. A group left with one session
turns back into a tab, and a deactivated session leaves its group.

Groups are the same in every browser and kept across restarts, in `~/.crew/voiceos/groups.json`.
Voice doesn't know about them.
