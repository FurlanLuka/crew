# v7.0.4: The bar where your thumb is

Two fixes for Voice OS on a phone.

The bar sits at the very bottom of the screen. In the app saved to an iPhone's home screen it could
sit well above the bottom when the app opened and after each screen change, until the keyboard had
been up once. The page is now pinned to the screen's edges, and only follows the visible part while
the keyboard is up. The phone also notes where the page and the bar end up in Voice OS's log, so if
it ever happens again the numbers say why.

No keyboard for nothing. With voice off, or in Text only, the text box only shows inside a session.
On Home or in Settings the bar says to open one, and with voice off it has a **Turn voice on**
button.
