# v6.10.0: Prompts you can read at a glance, and sessions that ask each other

## Prompts

Everything a session waits on now docks above the text box as one raised card: a permission, a
question, a plan, a `/clear` or `/compact` to confirm, a switch, a call auto mode blocked, and
another session's request. The title asks the question, a grey line beside it says why, then comes
what you're judging and the answers, with the main one last. One dim line says what to say to answer
it by voice.

A permission shows the command with each program it runs picked out, and **Always allow** names what
it would remember (`bun test`, say, or "+1 more" when it remembers more). **Deny and say why…** opens a
field in the card for your reason. A question lists its options as rows; **Something else…** opens a
field for your own answer, and a question that takes several answers lets you pick more than one,
then **Send**. A plan scrolls in its card, **Read it all** makes it as tall as the card allows, and
**Change the plan…** opens a field to send it back.

Queued messages fold into the text box as **2 queued**, with an amber dot when one came from another
session; click it to see them with ▲ now and ✕ cancel. Voice OS's last spoken line is a quiet caption
over the box. Esc in the small menus closes just the menu, and Space on a card's button clicks it
instead of starting the mic.

## Sessions asking sessions

When a session asks another one something, the asking session keeps the exchange as a card in its
conversation: waiting with the other side's live step, then the answer and the files it read, with
**View steps** for everything the copy did. The asked session shows the question in its side panel,
under **asked by other sessions**, and its steps window fills in live. A message one session hands
another arrives marked as theirs and goes from **Queued** to **Delivered** on the sender's card. A
secret shows a lock and its name, then **Copied** with the private file it went to.

## Model and working line

Beside the permission chip a second chip says which model the session runs on ("Opus 5.5 · 1M"). Tap
it to switch between Opus, Sonnet and Haiku for the next turn. While a turn runs the session shows it
is at work for the whole of it: a running tool has its own line with a clock, thinking has a line at
the foot of the stream, and when it needs you that line says so in red.

## Home

With tab groups, Home shows one list per group, in the group's colour and the bar's order, waiting
sessions first and a red count on the header when one needs you. Sessions outside any group list last.
Click a group's header to open it.

## Set up

The first run shows once. It offers Import, Set it up step by step, or Skip, and a skipped Mac gets an
empty board to start from. Workspaces are the first tab, a workspace's page shows its worktrees as
cards with their servers, and a worktree's Dev servers card starts, stops, restarts and verifies in
place, with a server's log beside the list. Import and the first run's scan clone what they find. While
Setup with Claude works on a worktree, that worktree's servers show beside the chat, and an @ menu
completes worktrees and projects. `crew setup logs <ref> <project>/<server>` prints what a server
printed while setup or verify tried it.
