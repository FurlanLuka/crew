# v5.1.0: Dictation

This one adds dictation, a fourth listening mode for when you want to think out loud. Click the mic
or press Space and just talk. Pauses don't end it and "stop" is only a word. When you hit **Send**,
the whole thing goes word for word to the session on screen, with a note that it was dictated, and
the router never touches it. A new press, closing the tab or switching modes sends it too, so the
only way to lose it is **Discard**, and that asks first once there are words.

I also tightened what happens to normal turns. The kernel picks where your words go, and Voice OS
sends them as you said them. It may only pass along a part when you did more than one thing in one
breath, and even then the part has to be copied word for word. The bottom bar got redone as well:
a round listening-mode button with its own menu, and a text box that grows to six lines, where Enter
sends and Shift+Enter adds a newline.
