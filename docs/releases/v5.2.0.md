# v5.2.0: Talking to sessions off screen

The big change here is how Voice OS handles talking to sessions that aren't on screen. Say
"checkout, is the build green?" and you stay where you are. Voice OS says "Sent to checkout", the
answer plays in full with its name on it, and follow-ups keep going there for a minute. Switches are
said out loud now, "go back" walks your last five screens, and "where am I?" gets an answer. Updates
from other sessions no longer talk over you. They wait for a quiet moment and come together as one
"Meanwhile, …" line, and "what did I miss?" plays them right away.

Voice OS also works in any language now. Every place that read what you meant with an English
pattern (a yes, a stop, "mute that", "for checkout?") asks a small Haiku judge instead, and when it
can't tell or times out it takes the safe side: nothing gets approved and no words get dropped.
There's a "Languages you speak" picker in the listening menu that feeds the hints to Soniox. The
speech layer itself (stop words, "end of turn", the wake word) stays English on purpose.

Two smaller things worth knowing. Permission asks no longer read the whole command at you, paths and
all. You hear "run grep" or "edit App.tsx", and the page still shows the full command. And if a
remote machine is on an older release, the main now runs `crew update` there over SSH and
reconnects, once per version, instead of refusing it. A remote that's newer than the main is never
downgraded; the card tells you to update this machine instead. I also fixed the live transcript
showing a local session with another machine's prefix, and a few judge regressions the evals caught
around mute and listening.
